## OBJECTIVE

  - The objective of this lab is to understand how to configure VLANs on Cisco Switches and also familiarize with the commands on Cisco IOS to validate and check configurations.  

## PURPOSE

  - VLANs allow for segmentation of network and multiples or broadcast domains. 

## CONFIGURATION 
  - configured HOSTNAME
  - configured VLAN
  - used <show vlan brief> to verify vlan
  - configured access port and assigned vlans
  - verified vlan and access port 

## TECHNOLOGY USED
 - Cisco Packet Tracer
 - Cisco Layer 2 access Switch
 - Endpoint PCs
 - Manual static IP Addressing

## DESIRED OUTCOME
 - Pinged PC 2 (in VLAN 10) from PC 1 (in VLAN 10) and it was successful.

 - Pinged PC 4 (in VLAN 20) from PC 2 (in VLAN 10) and it was unsuccessful.
